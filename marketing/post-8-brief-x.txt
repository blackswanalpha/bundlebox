## Thread

1/
Ask an AI coding assistant to fix one bug and watch what it does first.

It opens file after file and reads them top to bottom. Sometimes twice. You pay for all of it.

2/
AI assistants charge by the word they read. Most of your bill is the AI looking around, not the AI writing the fix.

3/
So I built bundlebox. It does the looking on your own computer, for free.

Then it gives the AI one short note: which files matter, which lines, and how to check the fix worked.

4/
I tried it on 12 real tasks.

Without bundlebox, the AI read 866,600 tokens. $4.33.
With it, 32,700 tokens. $0.16.

Same tasks. About 26x less reading.

5/
The note also says what it doesn't know. If there is no test to check the fix, it says so instead of guessing.

One question is cheaper than one wrong assumption.

6/
One caveat: those 12 tasks came from bundlebox's own project, so it had home advantage. On an outside benchmark it found the right files more often but read more to do it. I publish both.

7/
Works with Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Copilot and more. Free and open source.
